# Risk and Issue Management Guide

## Risk vs Issue

A risk is something that may happen.

An issue is something that has already happened or is actively affecting the project.

## Example Risks

- Permit approval may be late
- Equipment shipment may slip
- Crew availability may become limited

## Example Issues

- Equipment delivery is already delayed
- Assigned installation crew is unavailable
- Survey engineer has a scheduling conflict

## Issue Management Process

### 1. Identify the Problem

Document the issue clearly.

### 2. Identify the Affected Site

Record which site is impacted.

### 3. Identify the Affected Milestone

Determine where the issue impacts the schedule.

### 4. Estimate Schedule Impact

Estimate the number of days affected.

### 5. Assign Ownership

Identify who is responsible for resolving the issue.

### 6. Create Corrective Action

Examples:

- Reassign resources
- Resequence work
- Confirm new delivery date
- Escalate permit status
- Schedule alternate reviewer

### 7. Track to Closure

Maintain the action in the action log until completed.
